# Dealdeck - 3 minute demo script

Open on the MacBook: https://tobi-room-desktop.tailaba628.ts.net:8443 (tailnet, mic works).
Pre-built decks (instant): see "Recent decks" on the landing page.

0:00  Hook. "A first look at a company costs an associate hours: pull Dealroom, PitchBook, LinkedIn,
      write the memo, build the deck. [Use the hours number the Phoenix Court VCs told you.]
      Dealdeck does it from one search box, on live Dealroom data."
0:15  Landing -> type "Synth" -> autocomplete (logo, HQ) -> Enter. Slides stream in while you talk:
      "it's calling ~10 Dealroom endpoints in parallel: profile, rounds, investors, team, similar
      companies, headcount, web traffic, news." Cover art is generated by the TwoShot API.
0:50  Click any number's citation chip -> the receipt: exact Dealroom endpoint + raw JSON.
      "Nothing on a slide is unsourced."
1:05  Voice (mic button): "Who are their three closest competitors and how much have they raised?"
      -> it queries Dealroom live, answers out loud, and ADDS a competitors slide while it talks.
1:40  "Bull vs Bear" -> two voices argue the deal for ~30s (stop after two exchanges).
2:05  Thesis: "Find companies for my thesis" -> pick LocalGlobe as "your fund" (or type a thesis)
      -> 6 companies it doesn't already own, each with why it fits -> click one -> memo gets a
      "Thesis fit" slide.
2:30  URL trick: take any Dealroom URL, put our host in front (or with the domain:
      app.dealroom.co -> app.dealroomdeck.co) -> deck builds.
2:45  Share -> QR on screen: "scan it, ask it your own questions; your questions fork a private copy."
3:00  "Built in 2.5 hours on the Dealroom API, TwoShot's generation API, Claude and ElevenLabs."

Fallbacks: if the venue wifi or a live build stalls, open a pre-built deck from Recent and demo
Q&A / Bull vs Bear / Present on it.
